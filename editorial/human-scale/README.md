# At Human Scale

This directory is the durable workspace for Morning Edition's recurring **At Human Scale** lane.

The canonical selection and writing rules live in [`HUMAN_SCALE_NEWS_PROTOCOL.md`](../../HUMAN_SCALE_NEWS_PROTOCOL.md). That root protocol remains authoritative.

## Purpose

This lane exists to preserve perspective by regularly including one or two low-temperature, genuinely local stories about ordinary life: towns, farms, schools, libraries, clubs, neighborhood institutions, local infrastructure, animals, crafts, community events, and other human-sized developments.

Its value is not comedy, virality, sentimentality, or global consequence. Its value is scale.

It is also deliberately a **window into ordinary life outside the United States**. U.S. stories are allowed, but the normal search and selection priority is non-U.S., including original-language local reporting when practical. The lane should avoid becoming either small-town America or a narrow circuit of easy English-language sources.

## Geographic working goal

Use this workspace to help maintain geographic range over time.

- Search outside the United States first.
- Aim for most published Human Scale selections to be non-U.S.; roughly 5 of 7 is a useful rolling benchmark, not a quota.
- Avoid consecutive U.S. selections when a credible non-U.S. candidate clears the bar.
- Rotate across world regions and languages rather than repeatedly using Britain, Canada, Australia, or other easy English-language ecosystems.
- Track source ecosystems that make high-quality ordinary local reporting discoverable in less-covered places.

The goal is ordinary life, not exoticism. A non-U.S. story should be selected because it is a strong human-scale story, not because the place appears quaint or unfamiliar.

## What belongs in this directory

Use this directory for durable supporting material that helps the lane improve over time without bloating the canonical protocol, for example:

- source-discovery notes for local and regional publications;
- geographic or language source lists, with particular attention to non-U.S. local reporting;
- rolling geographic-balance observations;
- examples of especially good human-scale selections;
- anti-patterns and rejected-story examples;
- recurring search strategies;
- editorial observations about what makes the section feel genuinely local rather than manufactured;
- future evaluation notes about whether the lane is providing the intended change of pace and geographic widening.

Do not store daily published stories here. Published human-scale items belong in the normal edition data and archives alongside the rest of Morning Edition.

## Publication location

In an edition, use a normal section object:

- title: `At Human Scale`
- recommended id: `at-human-scale`
- normal size: 1–2 concise items

The website is data-driven, so this section does not require a separate hard-coded page or renderer.
