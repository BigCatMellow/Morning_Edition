## In Case You Missed It

### Brazil records its lowest annual Amazon deforestation since satellite monitoring began
PRODES measured 3,335 square kilometres cleared from August 2025 through July 2026, a reduction of about 42%. Fire and forest degradation remain separate problems.

**Why it matters:** A measurable conservation gain matters, but no single metric captures the Amazon's condition.

*Folha de S.Paulo — 2026-10-16*

### Older catch-up: scientists find separate early developmental pathways for brain regions
A September study traced distinct embryonic origins for the forebrain/midbrain and hindbrain, then generated hindbrain motor neurons from stem cells.

**Why it matters:** The result may improve neurological research; it does not establish that humans have two independent minds.

*Stanford Medicine — 2026-09-18*

## Outside the Bubble

### An ultracold gas makes a 1931 quantum prediction testable
Researchers formed and probed Bethe strings, bound groups of atoms in an effectively one-dimensional gas.

**Why it matters:** A controlled platform for many-body physics is a discovery worth understanding, without speculative product claims.

*University of Innsbruck — 2026-09-14*

### Awkwardness may reveal broken social scripts, not awkward people
