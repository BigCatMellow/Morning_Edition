# Morning Edition — November 11, 2026

Abu Dhabi peace talks; climate commitments in Antalya; school inequality; and a London parakeet debate.

## Lead Story

### Ukraine and Russia enter a second day of US-mediated talks without a breakthrough
Russian, Ukrainian and US delegations continued talks in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday. Humanitarian exchanges and security guarantees are on the agenda, but territory and a ceasefire remain deeply contested while attacks continue.

**Why it matters:** The immediate test is whether diplomacy can produce prisoner returns or verifiable restraint before the parties resolve the war's central disputes.

## Front Page

### COP31 leaders face a widening gap between climate promises and action
At the Antalya climate summit, negotiators are debating electrification and implementation while emissions and adaptation funding remain inadequate. The US is absent; Turkey proposes increasing electricity's share of energy use, without necessarily requiring that power to be clean.

**Why it matters:** The meeting's significance will be judged by measurable commitments, finance and follow-through rather than summit declarations.

*Associated Press / Reuters — 2026-11-11*

### Canada restores sanctions against 11 Iranian entities
Canada reimposed measures against 11 Iranian organizations, including banks and energy firms, alleging links to nuclear and missile activity. Tehran says its nuclear program is peaceful; the move comes amid regional conflict and possible diplomatic openings.

**Why it matters:** Sanctions change financial access and diplomatic leverage, but their effects on nuclear negotiations remain uncertain.

*Reuters — 2026-11-10*

### A British study finds a structural disadvantage in selective-school admissions
Researchers using Kent data report that disadvantaged pupils with comparable attainment are less likely to take and pass grammar-school entrance tests. The authors argue that opt-out testing alone would not resolve the underlying inequity.

**Why it matters:** The results distinguish a visible procedural barrier from deeper inequality established before an exam is taken.

*Phys.org / British Journal of Sociology of Education — 2026-11-10*

## In Case You Missed It

### A six-week job-search intervention shows promising trial results
In a randomized study of 126 unemployed adults with elevated depressive symptoms, 68.3% using a mental-health and job-coaching app found work versus 50.8% in a comparison group. Larger studies are needed.

**Why it matters:** A practical intervention may address emotional barriers to employment alongside job-search tactics, but short trials cannot establish lasting outcomes.

*Northwestern University / Journal of Medical Internet Research — 2026-11-10*

### Older catch-up: smaller particle collisions reveal signs of primordial plasma
Four LHC experiments reported complementary evidence that collisions involving oxygen and neon can create quark-gluon plasma, a state of matter associated with the early universe. The findings include preliminary analyses.

**Why it matters:** The work broadens the kinds of collisions physicists can use to investigate matter under extreme conditions.

*CERN / ScienceDaily — 2026-11-02*

## At Human Scale

### A New Jersey library invites neighbors to celebrate Diwali together
Parsippany's public library scheduled its free annual Diwali gathering for November 7, with music, dance, henna, crafts and food. Organizers described it as a way for families and neighbors to share traditions.

**Why it matters:** A neighborhood library can become a small civic meeting place across generations and cultures.

*RLS Media / Parsippany Library — 2026-10-26*

## Ideas

### Should we retire the concept of knowledge?
Philosopher David Papineau argues that the traditional demand for knowledge, rather than well-supported true belief, can distort decisions and communication. His provocative examples test whether statistical evidence is unfairly discounted.

**Why it matters:** The essay asks what evidence should license action when absolute epistemic security is unavailable.

*Aeon / David Papineau — 2019-06-01*
