Leo's first visit to Argentina follows his stop in Uruguay and comes amid disagreements between church leaders and President Javier Milei about poverty, government spending and the Iran war. Their public encounter has not yet occurred.

## Front Page

### Russian drone strikes rescuers at site of deadly Ukrainian shelter attack
Ukraine's emergency service says a Russian drone damaged rescue equipment at Komyshuvakha while crews worked at a former school and aid shelter struck earlier in the week. No rescuers were reported injured in the latest strike; Ukraine says the earlier attack killed at least 55.

**Why it matters:** Repeated attacks at a disaster scene complicate search and identification work; casualty figures and attribution are based on Ukrainian authorities' reporting.

*Associated Press — 2026-11-08*

### Iran-war uncertainty rattles Gulf markets
Saudi and Qatari stock indexes fell Sunday as traders weighed renewed reports of attacks around Iran, the U.S. blockade and possible escalation. Some claims about mine strikes and explosions remain unverified or contested.

**Why it matters:** The regional financial response reflects fears that a conflict around vital shipping routes could intensify and affect energy supplies.

*Reuters — 2026-11-08*

### Astronomers find two rings of planetary debris around a dead star
Observations of white dwarf J1328−1450 reveal distinct rings of rocky debris with different magnesium signatures. Researchers propose disrupted planetary bodies as a possible explanation, but the history of the material remains uncertain.

**Why it matters:** The system provides a rare observational window into the aftermath of planetary systems as their stars age.

*Reuters / Nature Astronomy — 2026-11-07*

## In Case You Missed It

### Greenland's parliament backs a security deal with Denmark and the United States
Greenlandic lawmakers approved an agreement allowing expanded defense cooperation while retaining Greenland's self-government and Danish ties. Danish parliamentary consideration remains necessary.

**Why it matters:** The debate is about security and Greenlanders' political agency, not merely the strategic preferences of larger countries.

*Associated Press — 2026-11-04*

### Older catch-up: new Martian gas observations raise possibility of ongoing volcanism
Scientists reported hydrogen chloride over fissures near Alba Mons and suggested it might reflect underground magmatic activity. The proposed mechanism is an interpretation, not a confirmed observation of erupting magma.

**Why it matters:** The finding could revise assumptions about Mars's internal activity and the environments where subsurface water might persist.

*Reuters / Science Advances — 2026-11-04*

### What if doing the right thing means choosing among several good things?
Philosopher Irene McMullin argues that moral conflict often comes from competing legitimate demands on limited time and attention, not a simple battle between duty and selfish desire. She proposes evaluating choices in the context of a whole life.

**Why it matters:** The essay offers a framework for moral tradeoffs when family, work, community and personal projects all make reasonable claims.
