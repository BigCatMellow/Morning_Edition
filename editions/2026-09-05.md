# Morning Edition — September 5, 2026

Global food prices climb to their highest level since 2022, autonomous-weapons talks stall over binding limits, Colombia and the United States deepen counter-cartel military cooperation, and an eight-letter synthetic genetic alphabet proves compatible with a natural transcription enzyme.

## Lead Story

### Global food prices reached their highest level since late 2022 as weather and war tightened supply expectations

The UN Food and Agriculture Organization says its Food Price Index averaged 133.3 points in August, up 1.9% from July and 2.5% from a year earlier. All five major commodity groups rose, with sugar up 11.9%; cereal prices also climbed as hot and dry weather, Black Sea export disruption and broader geopolitical risk tightened expectations for supply.

**Why it matters:** The index remains about 17% below its March 2022 peak, so this is not yet a repeat of that crisis. The important signal is that climate, war and trade risks are pushing in the same direction at once.

Source: [FAO / Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/world-food-prices-highest-since-2022-supply-risks-mount-fao-says-2026-09-04/)

## Front Page

### Autonomous-weapons talks are stuck over whether human-control rules should be legally binding

Negotiators from 128 states remain divided over whether lethal autonomous weapons should face a binding treaty or non-binding guidance under existing humanitarian law. The institutional question is whether enforceable limits arrive before procurement and doctrine become entrenched.

Source: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/opposition-by-us-russia-raises-doubts-over-autonomous-weapons-talks-outcome-2026-09-04/)

### Colombia and the United States are rebuilding a deeper counter-cartel military partnership

Southern Command and Colombia's new government are expanding cooperation around Pacific maritime surveillance, intelligence sharing, the Ecuador border and military-aircraft readiness. The partnership may strengthen state capacity while raising questions about sovereignty, lethal-force rules and militarization of drug policy.

Source: [El País](https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-09-02/us-launches-new-military-alliance-with-colombia-focused-on-pacific-narco-boats-and-the-border-with-ecuador.html)

### Vietnam and Myanmar agreed to make defence and security cooperation a major pillar of their relationship

Vietnam and Myanmar will expand military training, officer exchanges, disaster response and information sharing while deepening economic ties. The agreement shows how uneven ASEAN's approach to Myanmar remains.

Source: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/vietnam-myanmar-agree-deepen-defence-ties-during-min-aung-hlaing-visit-2026-09-05/)

### The UN General Assembly endorsed Equal Earth-style world maps that show Africa at its actual relative scale

A Togo-led resolution backed by the African Union won 164 votes and encourages general-purpose maps to move away from Mercator toward equal-area projections. The resolution is non-binding, but adoption by schools, media and digital platforms could reshape geographic intuition.

Source: [EFE](https://efe.com/english/science-and-technology/2026-09-04/un-adopts-resolution-to-use-map/)

## Science

### A natural bacterial enzyme can transcribe an eight-letter synthetic genetic alphabet

Researchers in *Nature Communications* showed that *E. coli* RNA polymerase can transcribe DNA containing two synthetic base pairs alongside the four natural DNA letters. This is an enzyme-level synthetic-biology result, not the creation of a viable eight-letter organism.

Source: [Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76668-0)

## In Case You Missed It

### France's Constitutional Council upheld the core of its assisted-dying law while giving private institutions a collective conscience exemption

France validated the central patient right created by its new assisted-dying law but allowed private and nonprofit institutions to refuse to host the procedure. The next test is whether a national right remains practically accessible when local institutions object.

Source: [Le Monde](https://www.lemonde.fr/societe/article/2026/08/14/le-conseil-constitutionnel-valide-la-loi-sur-la-fin-de-vie-les-etablissements-catholiques-obtiennent-leur-clause-de-conscience_6746261_3224.html)

### Mexico widened anti-money-laundering rules beyond banks to crypto, casinos, real estate, art and other high-risk sectors

Mexico expanded customer-identification, transaction-monitoring and beneficial-ownership requirements across sectors commonly used to move or conceal illicit funds. The reform's value now depends on enforcement capacity.

### Should moral and political concepts be judged partly by whether they actually help people solve social problems?

A Notre Dame Philosophical Reviews essay on Matthew Lindauer's *The Fruitfulness of Normative Concepts* asks whether ideas such as justice, responsibility and solidarity should be evaluated partly by whether they motivate good action, resist bias, support consensus and guide behavior.

Source: [Notre Dame Philosophical Reviews](https://ndpr.nd.edu/reviews/the-fruitfulness-of-normative-concepts/)
