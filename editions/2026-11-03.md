## Something to Sit With

> The world is too much with us; late and soon, / Getting and spending, we lay waste our powers:

*— William Wordsworth · The World Is Too Much With Us*

In this 1807 sonnet, Wordsworth argues that commerce and routine can crowd out attention to nature. The lines criticize narrowed perception, not the necessity of work.

**A question to carry:** What have you stopped noticing because your days are full of getting things done?
