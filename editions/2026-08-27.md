### A 324-million-year-old insect fossil still carried paddle-like abdominal legs
*peer-reviewed research · Nature · United Kingdom / international · 2026-08-26*

Researchers describe Chosha praecursor, a Carboniferous fossil from Texas that has clear insect features but also multisegmented abdominal legs with paddle-like modifications. Their phylogenetic reconstruction places it among early-diverging insects and suggests that at least some stem insects were semiaquatic before the modern six-legged body plan was fully assembled.

**Why it matters:** The fossil helps fill the long ‘hexapod gap’ between molecular estimates of insect origins and an unusually sparse early fossil record. The anatomy is directly preserved, while the exact evolutionary placement and semiaquatic interpretation remain reconstructions that will be tested against future fossils.
