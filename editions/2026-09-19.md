# Morning Edition — September 19, 2026

## Lead
### A coordinated assault on a police compound in northwest Pakistan killed at least 31 people
An explosives-laden vehicle struck the Kohat Police Lines compound during Friday prayers, followed by gunmen entering the site. Reuters, citing AFP and a police official, put the toll at 31 early Saturday; Pakistani outlets reported lower confirmed hospital counts while a roughly 21-hour clearance operation ended with eight attackers killed.

**Why it matters:** The attack hit police and counterterrorism infrastructure in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, where militant violence has intensified. The exact casualty count remains fluid, and responsibility was not independently established at publication.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/death-toll-31-pakistan-police-compound-attack-afp-reports-2026-09-19/

## Front Page

### Bolivia ended its diesel subsidy and tied the pump price to international import costs
The government set diesel at 17.95 bolivianos a litre from Saturday, sharply above the subsidized price, using a formula linked to import parity. The change followed congressional approval of a $1.9 billion IMF program that still requires IMF Executive Board approval.

**Why it matters:** The reform targets a costly subsidy and chronic shortages but immediately raises transport and production costs; the government announced cash and credit measures to cushion the shock.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/bolivia-scraps-diesel-subsidy-combat-fuel-shortages-2026-09-19/

### A Chinese coast-guard vessel and a Philippine fisheries ship collided near Palawan; each side blames the other
BRP Datu Magat Salamat was delivering fuel to fishermen about 54 nautical miles off Palawan when it collided with China Coast Guard vessel 21585. Manila says the Chinese ship deliberately rammed it; Beijing says the Philippine vessel cut across its bow. No injuries were reported.

**Why it matters:** The collision is another physical confrontation in a disputed sea where routine fisheries missions can become state-to-state security incidents. Contact and damage are established; intent remains contested.

Source: https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/09/19/china-vessel-rams-and-damages-philippine-fishing-boat-coast-guard-says

### Malaysia’s governing coalition is straining after Najib Razak was moved toward house arrest
Former prime minister Najib Razak received a conditional royal pardon allowing the remainder of his current sentence to be served under house arrest until August 2028, subject to a RM50 million fine. DAP leader Anthony Loke then offered his resignation in protest.

**Why it matters:** Najib’s 1MDB-linked convictions helped reshape Malaysian politics. The pardon puts anti-corruption credibility and coalition stability in tension inside Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim’s government.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/malaysias-democratic-action-party-leader-resigns-cabinet-2026-09-19/

## In Case You Missed It

### Kenyan traders pushed the government to reverse a customs change that had triggered street protests
After the revenue authority raised the benchmark for consolidated cargo, small traders protested and police used tear gas. Within days, the government agreed to cut the general benchmark to 2 million shillings and separate ordinary goods from higher-value merchandise.

**Why it matters:** It shows how a technical customs rule can hit thousands of small importers who share containers—and how organized commercial pressure can force rapid policy redesign.

Source: https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/2026-09-04-state-bows-to-traders-pressure-cuts-cargo-benchmark-to-sh2m

## Ideas

### What disappears when we treat all knowledge as something that can be computed?
Ingrid Burrington criticizes “computism”: treating knowledge, taste and culture as if their highest form were machine-readable information optimized independently of the people who know, judge and experience things.

**Why it matters:** The hard question is whether some knowledge is inseparable from situated judgment and embodiment—or whether that claim romanticizes human judgment while understating what formal systems can reveal.

Source: https://aeon.co/essays/if-computers-count-all-the-petals-who-tastes-the-cherries

## At Human Scale

### Tarama Island is spending this weekend dancing for the harvest
Tarama Village in Okinawa is holding its three-day Hachigatsu Odori harvest festival, with neighborhood performances of Ryukyuan court dance and kumiodori; the tradition is an Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property of Japan.

**Why it matters:** Its stakes are intentionally local: an island community keeping a harvest ritual alive through performance and neighborhood participation.

Source: https://www.iwafu.com/en/events/1156433

## Worth Your Time

### Sometimes paying more attention makes us know less
A Psyche essay connects experiments in perception with philosophical contextualism: attention is a selection mechanism, not a guarantee of accuracy, and intense scrutiny can sometimes distort perception or raise skeptical standards until ordinary knowledge feels impossible.

**Why it matters:** It reframes attention as a limited cognitive allocation system. The useful question is not whether attention is good, but when additional attention stops improving a judgment.

Source: https://psyche.co/ideas/sometimes-paying-attention-means-we-see-the-world-less-clearly
