*science reporting · Nature · United Kingdom · 2026-08-25*

**Why it matters:** The study adds unusually detailed human evidence to a long-running debate over whether adults continue making hippocampal neurons and how that process might relate to depression. ## One Thing to Think About

### Can a democracy fail from incapacity before it fails at the ballot box?
*editorial question · Morning Edition*

We usually imagine democratic decline as a leader breaking rules, corrupting elections or silencing opponents. But there is another route: institutions remain formally democratic while becoming too slow, brittle or dysfunctional to carry out ordinary public decisions.

**Why it matters:** Legitimacy depends on more than choosing leaders. It also depends on whether institutions can turn collective decisions into competent action. Which failure ultimately does more damage: power that becomes less accountable, or government that remains accountable but can no longer reliably do what it promises?
